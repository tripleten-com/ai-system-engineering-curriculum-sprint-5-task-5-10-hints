# Before you submit

The basic checks confirm that your work is there. They don't judge whether it's right. Your instructor reads your pull requests and evidence notes with four questions. Ask them of your own work first.

## Did you meet the Task's requirements?

- Did all four checks run to the end on `main`, against the candidate's build, with commands and times kept?
- Does the manifest hold exactly seven fields, six copied by a command, with the decision `pending`?
- Does your verifier compare the manifest with the candidate, name a wrong field, and fail against a changed copy?
- Does your note's overview list all ten Tasks with their pull requests and notes?

## Did you explain your design choices?

- Does your note say how the decision field is shaped and what the command does when the candidate doesn't verify?
- Does it say how you decided the candidate still describes `main`?

## Can your tests fail when the code is wrong?

- Did you let the check accept an eighth field, ignore one changed value and allow another decision once each, and see tests fail?
- Do your four answers name the breaks you made across Part 1?

## Did you state the limits of what your checks show?

- Does your note gather every stand-in and every limit from Tasks 1 to 10 in one list?
- Does it say where you used a solution guide, if you did?
